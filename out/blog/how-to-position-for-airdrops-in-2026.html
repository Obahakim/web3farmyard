How to position for Airdrops in 2026

By defiOG.eth
22 April 2026
Updated 22 April 2026

As a beginner or a seasoned OG, getting positioned to receive airdrops can be a problem that’s why you hear people saying airdrops are dead. They really are not. Projects will keep rewarding their earliest users, the only thing that will change or is changing is the mode the use in determining who is eligible or how to identify their earliest users. Now it is up to you as an airdrop hunter to find those methods and position properly.

There was a time when all you needed to do is just register an email and you’re rewarded as an early user. The waitlist era. There was a time when it required just few transactions on testnet and you’re rewarded. The testnet era. And it goes on. The meta keeps changing and to be honest it starts looking like it’s impossible but no. The System just got serious and people are panicking. Stay with me….
People noticed that just reposting and following and writing few tweets about a project, clicking testnets, grinding on discords is no longer enough and they panicked. But that’s not why we are here, how can we beat the odds and stay positioned?

This is OG and Beginner friendly mate, stay put.

1. Build a “Main Identity Wallet”

Use one serious wallet as your flagship ship as a beginner.
If you are an OG, you chose a steady wallet. The era of multiple wallet farming is gone, this is 2026.

This wallet should:

• Have transaction history • Hold some real assets • Use dApps naturally • Bridge occasionally • Vote when possible • Join communities linked to protocols

Projects increasingly reward believable users.

Do not look like a transaction bot.

2. Focus on Fresh Ecosystems, Not Dead Narratives. In other words, DYOR (do your own research)

Look for chains/ecosystems that are:

• New mainnets (if you have liquidity) or New testnets (if you need a 0$ participation)
• Strong funding rounds (amount raised from investors)
• Growing TVL (total value locked) [what is TVL?]
• No token yet. You need to be early enough to take part in TGE (Token generation event)
• Active users but not overcrowded.. but make sure to be prepared to miss out on good drops you thought were over populated. If you can, do everything that is not scam. [How to identify scam projects?]

Examples of categories to watch in 2026:

• New L2 / L3 ecosystems • Modular chains • AI x Crypto tooling • Privacy infrastructure (zero knowledge protocols) • Consumer crypto apps • DeFi with no token • Restaking / yield layers

3. Farm your Behavior, Not Transactions

Think of your wallet as a citizen, not a spam gun

It is a bad strategy to:

• Swap $1 twenty times in one hour

What will u rate yourself as, if you were the regulator?

Better thing to do is:

• Bridge in 2 or 3 times. • Swap couple of tokens. Say 5 times taking note of your observations (you’re supposed to be a tester) • LP for about a week • Return later • Use another feature • Vote (if available) • Refer one real user (try to. It’s not compulsory but it’s a signal that you’re really human) • Interact weekly or daily (but avoid spamming transactions at once)

This works for both testnet and mainnet farming.

And if the project has a discord community, give your feedback on how the dApp you are testing is. Proper feedback and if you find a bug, report it.

4. Become a Cross-Pollinated User

Protocols love users who came from elsewhere
Make friends from everywhere you can. Connect with web3 enthusiasts on X and lend your voice. You can grind alone and still lend your voice anonymously; That’s the essence of web3

Use:

• Multiple chains • Wallets • Bridges • Messaging layers • DeFi aggregators

Sometimes Being visible across ecosystems can matter more than raw volume.

Now if you do all these, you’ll be surprised how many airdrops you’ll be eligible for.

In conclusion I will tell you what to look out for

1: Being Early Before Influencers Arrive

2: Consistency
20 weeks of light activity is better than 1 weekend frenzy.

Reputation

3: GitHub, Discord contribution, content creation, bug reports, guides.

Many drops in 2026 rewards value creators. Take for instance Arc architect program and Verse8 gaming.

In brutal markets, many quit. That’s often when best positioning happens.
