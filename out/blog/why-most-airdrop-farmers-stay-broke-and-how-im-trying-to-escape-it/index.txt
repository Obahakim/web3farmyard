<img src="https://web3farmyard.com/cms/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-2.jpg" alt="Why Most Airdrop Farmers Stay Broke (and how i am trying to escape it)">Why Most Airdrop Farmers Stay Broke (and how i am trying to escape it)



<p>I won’t lie… this one pain me small to admit. I’ve been farming airdrops for a while. Doing testnets, interacting, keeping up with threads, trying not to miss opportunities. But one day, I had to ask myself one simple question:</p>



<p>Why am I still broke?</p>



<p>Because if we’re being honest, a lot of us are active in Web3… but nothing is really changing.</p>



<p>You wake up, check Twitter (X).<br>See one thread: “Don’t miss this airdrop.”<br>You rush go interact. Another one drops. You jump again. Before you know it, your day is full. But your wallet? Still looking at you like nothing happened. At some point I realized something, I was working… but I wasn’t progressing. E be like say I dey run race, but na treadmill.</p>



<p>The problem is not that airdrops don’t pay. We’ve seen people win.The real problem is how most of us are approaching it. Including me.</p>



<h3>1. Farming Without Structure</h3>



<p>At first, I was just jumping from one project to another. Anything wey trend, I go enter.<br>No plan, No system, No tracking. Just vibes and WiFi. It felt productive… but it wasn’t efficient.</p>



<h3>2. Ignoring Positioning</h3>



<p>This one changed everything for me. I used to think “As long as my wallet is active, I’m good.”<br>I didn’t know that Discord activity, Twitter presence, community engagement… all these things matter. Some people are not just farming.<br>They are visible, They show up, They engage, They get noticed.<br>Meanwhile, I was just another wallet address.</p>



<h3>3. Chasing Hype Instead of Timing</h3>



<p>This one is very common. You see a project trending… thousands of people already inside, you still go farm am. By the time rewards come, everything don dilute. You go still get something… but it’s dust.</p>



<h3>4. Inconsistency</h3>



<p>Some days, I go hard. 10 projects, Full energy.<br>Next few days? Nothing.No rhythm, no consistency. And in this game, consistency quietly beats intensity.</p>



<h3>5. No Personal contribution to the space</h3>



<p>I realized that if I don’t build something for myself, I’m always depending on luck.<br>So I started building. Writing. Documenting. Sharing what I’m learning. Not because I know everything… but because I’m figuring things out.</p>



<p>And that itself has value.</p>



<p>The Shift<br>I stopped treating airdrops like random opportunities and started treating it like a system. Now I think in layers. Right now, I’m:</p>



<pre><code>•   Farming (on-chain) → still important
•   Positioning (Discord, Twitter) → now intentional
•   Building (an onboarding platform) → long-term play</code></pre>



<p>No be only to show up… you gats show say you dey useful to the space. Onboard Normies in your area if you can. Talk about web3 or build if u can. One thing I had to accept:</p>



<p>Being busy is not the same as making progress.</p>



<p>That line alone should changed how you move.</p>



<p>My Take<br>Airdrops still pay but not for everyone and not in the same way. If you keep approaching it randomly, results will also be random. But if you start treating it like a system…</p>



<p>Things begin to change.</p>
