Luna Classic — The Ghost Chain After Death

What was Luna Classic (LUNC)?

After Luna crashed, the Terra blockchain continued to exist. The community renamed LUNA to LUNC (Luna Classic) in an attempt to rebuild. But the chain had lost all credibility.

What Went Wrong?

LUNC trades at $0.0001 with almost zero activity. The chain is effectively dead. It serves as a permanent reminder of what happens when a $40B ecosystem collapses. Very little development occurs. The community is essentially abandoned.

Lessons Learned

Collapse is permanent. Even if a chain technically survives, the loss of trust and developer ecosystem means functional death.
