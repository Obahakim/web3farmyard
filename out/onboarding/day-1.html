Create Your Wallet & Claim Your First Testnet ETH

You're taking your first step into Web3. Today you'll set up a secure wallet, learn how to use it, and claim free testnet ETH to practice with. No real money needed.

⏱️ 20 minutes
🎓 Educational
✓ Hands-on
1

Create or Import Your MetaMask Wallet

MetaMask is a self-custody wallet — you hold your own keys, not a company. It's the most popular Web3 wallet and works on all chains you'll use in this course.

📌 Important: Self-Custody vs Exchanges

When you create a wallet, you get a seed phrase (12-24 words). This is your ONLY way to recover your wallet if you lose access. Write it down on paper and store it somewhere safe. Never share it with anyone, including Web3Farmyard support.

  1. A.

    Visit metamask.io

    Download the browser extension or mobile app.

  2. B.

    Click "Create a New Wallet"

    Create a strong password (12+ characters, mix of letters/numbers/symbols).

  3. C.

    Save Your Seed Phrase

    Write down all 12 words in order. Store on paper in a safe place (NOT on your computer).

2

Learn How to Copy Your Wallet Address

Your wallet has an address — a long string of numbers and letters that you'll use to receive tokens. Your address is PUBLIC (safe to share). Your seed phrase is PRIVATE (never share).

📋 How to Copy Your Address:

  1. 1.Open MetaMask and look at the top of the screen
  2. 2.You'll see your account name (e.g., "Account 1")
  3. 3.Below it is your address (starts with 0x)
  4. 4.Click on the address to copy it

✓ Example: What Your Address Looks Like

0x742d35Cc6634C0532925a3b844Bc9e7595f0bEb

(This is a real example address format. Your address will be different.)

3

Claim Your First Testnet ETH

Testnet ETH is FREE and worthless in real life. It's ONLY for practice. You'll use it to practice swapping, bridging, lending, and interacting with dApps without any risk of losing real money.

Why Sepolia Testnet?

Sepolia is Ethereum's official testnet. It's free, fast, and safe for practicing. Most DeFi dApps (Uniswap, Aave, etc.) have Sepolia versions for testing.

To claim testnet ETH:

  1. A.

    Go to faucet.trade/sepolia-eth-faucet

    This is the most reliable free testnet faucet.

  2. B.

    Make sure you're on the Sepolia network in MetaMask

    Click the network dropdown at the top of MetaMask. If you don't see Sepolia, add it manually (we'll have a guide).

  3. C.

    Paste your wallet address in the faucet form

    This is the 0x... address you copied from MetaMask.

  4. D.

    Click "Send Me ETH" and wait

    It may take 30 seconds to 5 minutes to arrive. You'll see it in MetaMask once it arrives.

✓ You've Completed Step 3!

Once you see testnet ETH in your MetaMask, you're ready for tomorrow's lesson on swapping and bridging.
