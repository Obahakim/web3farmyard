Day 6: Complete a Quest

Finish one on-chain quest from Galxe or LayerZero to build your reputation.

Choose one quest platform:

🚀 Galxe.com (Recommended for beginners)✨ TaskOn.xyz (Earn real rewards)📊 Zealy.io (Build XP score)

What to do:

  1. Pick ONE of the platforms above
  2. Connect your wallet (MetaMask or OKX)
  3. Browse available quests
  4. Complete at least 1 quest (usually takes 5-15 minutes)
  5. Confirm completion and take a screenshot
