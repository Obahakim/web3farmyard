Why Mobile Airdrop Farming Works

Crypto airdrops distribute tokens to early users of a protocol — often worth hundreds or thousands of dollars. They reward on-chain activity: swapping, bridging, providing liquidity, or using a testnet. Most of these interactions work perfectly on mobile.

You do not need a $2,000 desktop setup. You need a strategy, a system, and the discipline to document your activity. The Mobile Farmer covers the exact workflow used by active airdrop hunters across three continents.

Specific to Mobile

Every instruction in this book has been tested on Android and iOS. Every wallet recommendation is mobile-compatible. Every protocol was accessed from a phone. If it cannot be done on a phone, it is not in this book.

Airdrop farming is one of the few genuine wealth transfer mechanisms in Web3 that does not require initial capital to work. The Mobile Farmer shows you how to maximise your chances of qualifying before the snapshot.
